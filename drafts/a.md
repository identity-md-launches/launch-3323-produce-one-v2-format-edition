LEAD:
ESMA calls for an end to services for noncompliant stablecoins. [https://www.esma.europa.eu/press-news/esma-news/esma-sets-out-supervisory-expectations-services-related-unauthorised]

The EU securities watchdog says licensed crypto firms should stop giving EU clients access to stablecoins that fail the bloc’s rules. It wants national supervisors to resolve any remaining legacy exposures by 8 January 2027. [https://www.esma.europa.eu/press-news/esma-news/esma-sets-out-supervisory-expectations-services-related-unauthorised]

Taking a stablecoin off the order book leaves open the question of what happens to the coins in customer accounts. The European Securities and Markets Authority’s 8 October 2026 opinion addresses both. Its guidance covers custody and transfers as well as trading, and asks national supervisors to check which services still give EU customers access. [https://www.esma.europa.eu/sites/default/files/2026-10/ESMA75-113276571-1742_Opinion_on_the_provision_of_crypto_asset_services_in_relation_to_non-MiCA-compliant_asset-referenced_tokens_and_e-money_tokens.pdf]

The European Commission’s earlier guidance dealt with services that amounted to offering a token to the public or admitting it to trading. ESMA now asks supervisors to look at what each service lets customers do, even when the service itself falls outside those two activities. It expects firms to put controls in place to prevent customers from acquiring the tokens or increasing existing positions. [https://www.esma.europa.eu/publications-data/questions-answers/2404] [https://www.esma.europa.eu/sites/default/files/2026-10/ESMA75-113276571-1742_Opinion_on_the_provision_of_crypto_asset_services_in_relation_to_non-MiCA-compliant_asset-referenced_tokens_and_e-money_tokens.pdf]

ESMA says a platform’s disclosures cannot substitute for safeguards that issuers must provide. Those safeguards include applicable redemption rights and requirements for reserves or the safekeeping of funds. In ESMA’s view, keeping a noncompliant token readily available also puts issuers that meet those obligations at a disadvantage. [https://www.esma.europa.eu/sites/default/files/2026-10/ESMA75-113276571-1742_Opinion_on_the_provision_of_crypto_asset_services_in_relation_to_non-MiCA-compliant_asset-referenced_tokens_and_e-money_tokens.pdf]

National supervisors can allow limited services where necessary to wind down existing positions. Customers could still be allowed to convert or withdraw balances, for example, under close supervision. ESMA asks supervisors to act as soon as possible once they find legacy exposures and sets 8 January 2027 as the outer limit for resolving them. [https://www.esma.europa.eu/press-news/esma-news/esma-sets-out-supervisory-expectations-services-related-unauthorised] [https://www.esma.europa.eu/sites/default/files/2026-10/ESMA75-113276571-1742_Opinion_on_the_provision_of_crypto_asset_services_in_relation_to_non-MiCA-compliant_asset-referenced_tokens_and_e-money_tokens.pdf]

[NEWS] Thailand issues rules for domestic crypto ETFs. [https://www.sec.or.th/EN/Pages/News_Detail.aspx?Lang=EN&NewsNo=216&NewsYear=2026&SECID=13411]
Thailand’s Securities and Exchange Commission issued rules on 8 October 2026 that take effect on 16 October 2026. A fund must track a single crypto asset and maintain average net exposure to it of at least 80% of net asset value across each accounting year. The rules also let Thai mutual and private funds invest in domestic crypto ETFs within existing limits. [https://www.sec.or.th/EN/Pages/News_Detail.aspx?Lang=EN&NewsNo=216&NewsYear=2026&SECID=13411]

[TECH] Ethereum developers defer a post-quantum signature proposal for Hegotá. [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0ZGRqRYac1k] [https://eipsinsight.com/calls/acde/247]
On the 8 October 2026 core developers’ call, Danno Ferrin argued for giving smart contracts a built-in way to check signatures from a lattice-based standard. [TECH] Solana activates the switch for shorter mainnet slots. [https://solana.com/news/solana-changelog-october-8-2026]
The Solana Foundation’s 8 October 2026 changelog reported that mainnet had activated the feature gate for reducing slots from 250 to 200 milliseconds. The shorter slots take effect one epoch after activation. The foundation said the Agave validator client would space snapshots further apart in slot count to keep full snapshots from becoming more frequent on the clock. [https://solana.com/news/solana-changelog-october-8-2026]
